HourOne Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

HourOne is an alarm clock. It has no accounts or sign-in, and the developer never sells your information. Your alarms and progress stay on your phone. The few times anything leaves the phone are listed below: the weather forecast, ads from Google, purchases through Google Play, app use measured with Google Analytics for Firebase, and the worldwide leaderboards if you choose to join them.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms, wake-up checks, timer and stopwatch settings, sounds, colors, fonts, clock faces, points, level, streaks, achievements, game scores (unless you join the worldwide leaderboards, below), your phoenix, and any time capsule messages and voice notes are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Android may include this data in your phone's own backup to your Google account, which Google encrypts and controls. If you make a HourOne backup file in Settings, it is encrypted and saved only where you choose.

What leaves your phone

Your location is rounded to about 10 km on the phone, then sent to MET Norway (api.met.no, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute's free weather service, no account) to get the day's forecast, at most about once a morning. HourOne keeps only the rounded position, on the phone. Weather data from MET Norway is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Platinum and higher members, and anyone who buys No ads in the Store, never see a banner. When a game ends, anyone can also choose to watch a short ad to continue the run; it only plays if you tap it. Ads never show on the ringing alarm, the morning screen or inside a game. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks first, and you can change your choice in Settings under Privacy choices for ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
PurchasesMemberships, 30-day passes and point packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; HourOne never sees your card or account details, only a confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it. See the Google Privacy Policy.
App use and purchases (Google Analytics for Firebase)From version 4.2.6, the Google Play version of HourOne uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a few moments in the app: your first game and games ending, mornings you get up without snoozing, when a special offer or the rating prompt is shown, and purchases (what was bought and its price). Firebase also records standard app events, such as the app being opened or updated and how long it is used, with the phone model, Android version, app version, language and an approximate region worked out from the IP address, under a random ID for this install and, where Android allows it, the phone's advertising ID. No name, email, alarm, message or game score is sent. The developer uses this to see how the app is used and, through Google Ads, to learn which ads lead to installs and purchases. HourOne turns ad personalization off for this data, and where the law asks for consent first (such as in the EU and UK) it stays off unless you agree on Google's consent screen; changing your choice in Settings under Privacy choices for ads switches it too. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
Worldwide leaderboards (only if you join)Nothing is sent until you choose to join on the WORLDWIDE tab of the Leaderboard. Then HourOne sends the name you pick (2 to 16 characters) and your best score in each game (and, from version 4.2.7, your Morning Brain score from the wake-up games) for today, this week, this season and all time, with the time it was posted, to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). They are stored under a random player ID that Firebase creates for this install: no email, no Google account, no sign-in. Your name and scores are shown to everyone who opens the worldwide boards. Google sees your phone's IP address when it connects, as with any internet request. To delete your name and scores from the boards, tap Change next to your name on the WORLDWIDE tab and pick Leave the boards. If you uninstall without leaving first, your entries stay on the boards; email the developer with your board name to have them removed. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
Messages you sendIf you send feedback or report a problem from the app, it opens your own email app with the message, the app version and your phone model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Morning coach

On phones that have Google's Gemini Nano, a short morning note is written by the model on the phone itself. Nothing you see in it, and nothing it is written from, leaves the phone.

Analytics and tracking

The Google Play version of HourOne measures app use and purchases with Google Analytics for Firebase, as described above; it has no other analytics or tracking tools. The other third-party services that receive data are Google AdMob, which serves the ads above, and Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), only if you join the worldwide leaderboards. If anything changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children

HourOne is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn location, calendar, camera or microphone off in your phone's settings; the features that need them simply hide or skip.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings under Privacy choices for ads, or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Leave the worldwide leaderboards at any time to delete your name and scores from them.
  • Delete everything on your phone by uninstalling HourOne.

Deleting your HourOne data

Most of what HourOne keeps is only on your phone. The worldwide leaderboards are the one place anything of yours is kept off it, and only if you joined them.

  • Worldwide leaderboards: open HourOne, go to the Leaderboard, open the WORLDWIDE tab, tap Change next to your name, then Leave the boards and Leave. Your name and every score you posted (today, this week, this season and all time) are deleted from the boards straight away. Nothing is kept.
  • Already uninstalled? Email the developer at the address shown on HourOne's Google Play Store page with the name you used on the boards, and your entries will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Everything on your phone (alarms, progress, points, streaks, game scores and the rest): uninstall HourOne, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
  • Ads data: held by Google AdMob, not by HourOne. Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, then Ads).
  • App use data: held by Google Analytics for Firebase under a random ID for your install, never with your name or email. Google deletes it automatically after its retention period, and uninstalling HourOne stops it. Resetting your advertising ID also breaks the link to it.
